Six-DOF Glider Simulator

A small unpowered sailplane, fully simulated in 6 degrees of freedom — roll, pitch, yaw, and 3D position.

  • Left stick — elevator (pitch) & ailerons (roll)
  • Right stick — rudder (yaw, left/right) & airbrake (drag down)
  • LAUNCH — release from tow at altitude
  • TILT — optional: steer by tilting your phone

Fly through all 5 rings, in order, to win. A new random course spawns every launch.

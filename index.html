Six-DOF Glider Simulator

A small motor glider — a light sailplane with a weak engine — fully simulated in 6 degrees of freedom: roll, pitch, yaw, and 3D position.

  • Left stick — elevator (pitch, inverted: down = nose up) & ailerons (roll)
  • Right stick — rudder (yaw, left/right) & airbrake (drag down)
  • THROTTLE — drag up/down to set engine power; holds its position
  • LAUNCH — release from tow at altitude, at full throttle
  • TILT — optional: steer by tilting your phone
  • SND — mute/unmute engine and effect sounds

Fly through all 8 rings, in order, then head back and land at the runway to win. A new random course spawns every launch. Watch the wind dial — gusty wind keeps shifting and will push you off course. Once all rings are cleared, a green RUNWAY dial appears showing the way home.
